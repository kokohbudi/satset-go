# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three roles share one app, no single dominant one:

- **Resellers / store owners** — run their store day-to-day: buy stock, sell top-ups, watch balance and transaction history. Often non-technical, on mobile, doing money transactions repeatedly and fast.
- **Internal admins** — manage the catalog (categories → products → denoms → meta), users, and resellers. Power-users who live in tables and forms.
- **End buyers** — purchase pulsa / data / game / PLN top-ups. Low frequency, want a fast, obviously-safe checkout.

Context is transactional and money-bearing: people come to do a task (buy, sell, top up, manage) and leave. Trust and speed beat exploration.

## Product Purpose

PPOB (Payment Point Online Bank) marketplace for digital products — pulsa, data packages, games, PLN. The app lets resellers transact and manage their store, admins manage the product catalog and users, and buyers complete top-ups. Success = a transaction that feels fast and unambiguously safe, and a catalog/admin surface efficient enough that managing it isn't a chore.

## Brand Personality

**Fast, trustworthy, clear.** Speed and reliability first — this handles money, so nothing should feel flimsy, playful, or uncertain. Confident and clean, not loud. The voice is plain and direct (Bahasa Indonesia), never cute. Warmth comes from the committed orange brand color and good typography, not from decoration.

## Anti-references

What this must NOT look like (all four explicitly rejected):

- **Generic SaaS template** — gradient hero-metric cards, identical icon grids, tiny tracked-uppercase eyebrows above every section, the AI-slop default.
- **Legacy PPOB panel** — dense 2010-era reseller dashboards: tiny text, table-soup, neon, zero whitespace.
- **Childish / over-gamified** — cartoon mascots, heavy gamification, anything that undermines trust for money handling.
- **Cold enterprise gray** — lifeless gray-on-gray corporate dashboards with no brand warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the feature.** Every money-bearing screen must read as safe and final — clear amounts, clear states, no ambiguity. Looking flimsy is a bug.
2. **Speed over exploration.** Users repeat the same tasks; optimize the common path to the fewest steps and the least thinking, not for discovery.
3. **Density without clutter.** Admins need a lot on screen; earn it with hierarchy and whitespace, never by shrinking text or stacking cards.
4. **Warm, not loud.** Carry the orange brand identity with intent; restraint is the default, color is earned emphasis.
5. **Mobile-real.** Resellers work on phones. The primary flows must be genuinely good at small sizes, not desktop layouts squeezed down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, visible focus states, keyboard-operable forms and tables.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation (crossfade / instant fallback).
- Touch targets ≥44px for reseller/buyer mobile flows.
- Bahasa Indonesia primary language; copy stays plain and literal.
